Frequently Asked Questions

Which grades are supported?

BloomQuest supports Grades 1–12.

Does the app require internet?

No. Once installed, BloomQuest can be used without an internet connection.

Can multiple children use the app?

Yes, the app supports multiple child profiles upto a maximum of two at this time.
