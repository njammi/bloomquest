Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new habit?

Tap the + button on the home screen, enter a name for your habit, choose an icon and color, set a reminder if you'd like, and tap Save.

How do I restore a lost streak?

Streaks are automatically tracked as long as you mark a habit complete each day. If you believe your streak was lost in error, please contact us with your account details and we'll look into it.

Can I sync my data across devices?

Yes! Sign in with your BloomQuest account and your habits and progress will sync automatically across all your devices.

How do I delete a habit?

Swipe left on the habit in the home screen and tap Delete, or open the habit and select Delete Habit from the settings menu.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to Settings → Notifications in the app to manage reminders, or use your device's system settings to manage app-level notifications.

How do I request deletion of my data?

You can delete your account from Settings → Account → Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data. You can also contact us directly to request data deletion.
