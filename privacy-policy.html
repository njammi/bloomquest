Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

BloomQuest respects your privacy and is committed to protecting children's information.

Information We Collect

BloomQuest is designed to minimize data collection. The app stores quiz progress, settings, and child profile information locally on the device only.

BloomQuest does not require users to create accounts and does not collect personal information such as full names, phone numbers, email addresses, or location data from children.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Parents are encouraged to supervise app usage.

Third-Party Services

BloomQuest does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or external data collection services.

Data Security

All app data is stored locally on the user's device. BloomQuest does not transmit user or child data to external servers.
